Who I Am

Hi, I'm T Devamani Raju

I'm a Data Engineer with experience working across modern data platforms and cloud technologies. I enjoy solving problems around scale, reliability and performance — turning messy, scattered data into something people can actually trust and use.

Outside of work, I'm equally shaped by the mountains I've trekked, the places I've traveled to, and the people and cultures I've encountered along the way. Bangalore is where I'm currently based.

This website is my attempt to bring both sides of my life — the engineer and the explorer — into one place.
