My Inspirations / My Family

My Family

Prasad Raju T · Vijaya · Neelima — the three people whose story made me who I am.

Who They Are

A Family Built on One Woman's Strength

My father, Prasad Raju T, passed away when I was just 14 days old. From that day, my mother, Vijaya, became both mother and father to my sister and me.

She took on every responsibility alone — raising two children, managing a household, and making sure both of us got an education, at a stage in life when most people would have needed support just to survive the loss themselves. She never let that become our burden.

My Mother

Vijaya — Both Mother and Father

Everything I am today, I owe to her.

Her Strength

Losing a husband at such a young age, with two infants to raise, is a weight most people never have to carry. My mother carried it without ever making my sister and me feel its absence. She worked to give us an education, made every decision alone, and quietly became the one constant in our lives.

I don't have memories of my father, but I have every memory of a mother who chose to be everything for us, every single day. That's not something I take lightly — it's the reason I want this page to exist, as a small way of showing my gratitude.

My Sister

Neelima — Growing Up Leaning on Each Other

We grew up as each other's support system.

Standing By Her

With our father gone and our mother carrying so much on her own, my sister and I learned early to look out for each other. As she grew up, I made it my responsibility as her brother to stand by her at every important stage of her life — and being able to fulfill that responsibility is one of the proudest moments of my life.

In Memory

Talabhatla Prasad Raju Garu

A father I never got the chance to know.

I was only 14 days old when he passed away, so I carry no memories of him — only the knowledge that his absence is what shaped the family my mother built in his place. This page is as much for him as it is for her and my sister.

Why They Inspire Me

What My Family Taught Me

  • 💪 Strength isn't the absence of hardship — it's showing up for the people who depend on you anyway.
  • 🙏 Gratitude for what one person sacrificed for you should be shown, not just felt.
  • 🤝 Being someone's support system is a responsibility worth taking seriously.
  • ❤️ Family isn't defined only by who's present — it's defined by who shows up.
