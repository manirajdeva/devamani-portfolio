Trekking Journal / Sivagange Hills Trek

Sivagange Hills Trek

Near Dobbaspet, Karnataka 4,488 ft Easy-Moderate
DifficultyEasy-Moderate
Trek Distance3–6 km
Highest Point4,488 ft
Duration1 Day
Visits3 Times

Sivagange is a rocky hill about 50–60 km from Bengaluru, near Dobbaspet — an easy-to-moderate day trek with a few steep, narrow sections near the summit, fitted with steps and railings. It's also known as "Dakshina Kashi" and is an important pilgrimage site.

The Route

Bengaluru → Tumkur Road / NH48 → Dobbaspet → Shivagange (about 7 km from Dobbaspet), and back the same way — a full day trip, there and back.

5:00 AM — Start From Bengaluru

Left Bengaluru early by road via Tumkur Road / NH48 through Dobbaspet toward Shivagange.

6:30 AM — Reach Shivagange

Breakfast near the base before starting — carrying water, wearing trekking shoes, sunscreen on, backpack light, and no loose food out in the open (monkeys are common on the hill).

7:00 AM — Start the Trek

The trail combines rocky terrain, stone steps, temples and viewpoints on the way up. Along the way:

  • 🛕 Shivaganga Temple
  • 🙏 Gangadhareshwara Temple
  • 💧 Olakala Teertha
  • 🛕 Honnammadevi Temple
  • 🐂 Nandi Statue
  • 🌄 Mountain viewpoints

9:00–9:30 AM — Reach the Summit

Took time at the top for the 360° views of the surrounding countryside, the rocky formations, the Nandi and temple surroundings, and some photography — a quiet break away from Bengaluru.

Difficult Moments

The final stretch near the summit gets steep and narrow — use the installed railings and take care. The descent puts real pressure on the knees on the steep stone steps, so it's worth taking it slow.

10:00 AM — Descending

Took the descent slowly with short breaks and stayed hydrated, given how much the steep stone steps can wear on the knees.

12:00 PM — Lunch & Return

Reached the base for lunch, rested for about 45–60 minutes, then started the drive back around 1:00 PM via Dobbaspet and NH48 — back in Bengaluru by mid-to-late afternoon.

Most Memorable Moment

Three Visits. One Mountain. Many Memories.

I have climbed Shivagange three times, and every visit felt different.

But the moment that stays with me most is reaching the higher sections of the hill, looking back at the trail I had just climbed, and seeing the landscape stretching far beyond the rocky slopes.

The interesting thing about Shivagange is that even though the mountain remains the same, you don't feel the same every time you climb it.

The first time was about discovering the trail.
The second time was about enjoying the experience.
The third time felt more like returning to a familiar place.

Sometimes, you don't climb a mountain to discover something new. You climb it to rediscover yourself.

Lessons From the Trek

Shivagange taught me that repeating a journey doesn't make it less meaningful.

Every climb depends on your mindset, your energy, the people you're with, the weather, and even the way you look at the trail.

A familiar mountain can still give you a completely different experience.

Three visits taught me to slow down, appreciate the journey, enjoy the small moments, and never underestimate a trail just because you've walked it before.

"The mountain didn't change. I did."

  • 💧 1.5–2 L water
  • 🧢 Cap
  • 🕶️ Sunglasses
  • 🧴 Sunscreen
  • 🧻 Tissues/wet wipes
  • 🎒 Small backpack
  • 🔋 Power bank
  • 📱 Phone/camera
  • 🩹 Basic first-aid
  • 🧂 ORS/electrolytes
  • 🍌 Light snacks (packed securely — monkeys are common on the hill)
