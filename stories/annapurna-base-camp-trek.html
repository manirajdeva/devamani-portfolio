The Plan

The Annapurna Base Camp trek is a classic Himalayan route into the Annapurna Sanctuary, a high glacial basin surrounded by Annapurna I (8,091 m), Annapurna South, Hiunchuli and the fishtail summit of Machapuchare. After the Karnataka treks and Kedarnath, this is the next big step: a multi-day trek at real altitude, with tea-house stops along the way.

April is one of the two best seasons for this trek — clear mornings, blooming rhododendron forests lower down, and stable weather for the final climb to base camp.

Typical Route

  • Pokhara → Nayapul → Ghandruk — start of the trail through terraced villages.
  • Chhomrong → Himalaya — descent and climb through bamboo and rhododendron forest.
  • Deurali → Machapuchare Base Camp — entering the glacial valley.
  • Annapurna Base Camp (4,130 m) — sunrise with the Annapurna range all around.
  • Return by the same valley, with an extra rest day built in if needed.

What To Watch For

  • Altitude sickness — ascend gradually and add acclimatisation days.
  • Long stone-step sections, especially around Chhomrong.
  • Cold mornings and quick weather changes near base camp.

Planning Checklist
